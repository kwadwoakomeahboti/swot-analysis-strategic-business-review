SWOT Analysis — Strategic Business Review

# Project Overview
This project presents a strategic SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) analysis designed to evaluate a business's competitive position and identify opportunities for sustainable growth.

# Business Objective
The goal of this project is to identify material strengths and weaknesses within the organisation's direct control, alongside key external opportunities and threats shaping the competitive landscape. The analysis concludes with evidence based strategic recommendations designed to address identified gaps and position EduFlex for long term, differentiated growth.

## SWOT ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK
### Strengths — Internal attributes that provide a competitive advantage 
### Weaknesses — Internal limitations that constrain performance or growth 
### Opportunities — External conditions the organisation can leverage for gain 
### Threats — External factors that could impair performance or market position

 ## STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
 Invest in Learner Engagement Infrastructure to Improve Course Completion Rates
 Consolidate STEM Specialisation and Build Institutional Partnerships
